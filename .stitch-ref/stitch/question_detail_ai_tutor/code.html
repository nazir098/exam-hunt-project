Consider a spherical Gaussian surface enclosing a point charge 'q' at its center. If the radius of the sphere is doubled, how does the total electric flux through the surface change?

info

Remember: The electric flux through a closed surface depends only on the charge enclosed by it.

Select One Option

Step-by-Step Solution

1

According to Gauss's Law, the net electric flux Φ through any closed surface is given by Φ = q_enclosed / ε₀.

2

Notice that the formula does not contain the radius 'r' of the spherical Gaussian surface.

3

Therefore, whether the radius is doubled or tripled, as long as the charge 'q' remains the same, the total flux through the surface remains unchanged.
